# Akame Ga Kill

**Genre:** Action, Dark Fantasy, Drama, Tragedy  
**Platform:** Netflix, Crunchyroll  
**Season:** 1

# Summary
Akame ga Kill! follows Tatsumi, a naive country swordsman who travels 
to the Imperial Capital to earn money for his starving village.
However, he quickly discovers that the city is deeply corrupt, ruled 
behind the scenes by a ruthless Prime Minister who manipulates a young Emperor.
After witnessing the capital's true horror, Tatsumi is recruited by Night Raid
a secret group of covert assassins working for the Revolutionary Army to overthrow the Empire.
